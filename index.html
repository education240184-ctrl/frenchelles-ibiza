Restaurants
  • Casa Maca
    A hidden hilltop escape overlooking Ibiza Town, known for its sunset dinners, 
    effortless atmosphere and perfectly curated crowd.
  • Jondal
    One of the most iconic beach restaurants in Ibiza, loved for its refined simplicity, crystal-clear water views and elevated Mediterranean dining.
  • Zuma Ibiza
    A globally renowned destination for contemporary Japanese dining, blending vibrant energy, signature cuisine and a glamorous evening scene.
  • Cala Bassa Beach Club
    A stylish beachfront spot with turquoise waters, relaxed luxury vibe and long sun-filled lunches that turn into unforgettable afternoons.
  • Cappuccino Marina
    The perfect sunset location overlooking Ibiza Marina, known for its golden hour atmosphere, elegant crowd and signature cocktails.
  • Blue Marlin Ibiza
    A legendary beachfront destination known for its vibrant energy, world-class DJs and seamless transition from daytime lounging to high-energy sunset parties.
  • Pacha Ibiza
    One of the most legendary clubs in the world, where iconic DJs like Marco Carola set the tone for Ibiza Opening nights.
  • UNVRS
    An exclusive new-generation club experience, featuring world-class artists like Black Coffee and a next-level Ibiza atmosphere.
